# Prototype controls

At startup, choose graphics, a hand style, and a mode for a new world. Select an existing world and click **Play selected world**, or enter a new name and numeric seed and click **Create world & play**. **Quick play** starts an unsaved world using the seed field. To return to the menu, close and restart the game.

- Click the window to capture the mouse; Escape releases it. Click again to resume.
- Mouse: look. W/A/S/D: move. Space: jump. Double-tap Space: toggle flight.
- In flight, hold Space to rise and Shift to descend. Hold Control to move faster.
- Left click: break a targeted block. Right click: place the selected block against the targeted face.
- Number keys 1–9 or mouse wheel: select a hotbar block.
- F1: Potato / Basic; F2: Normal; F3: High; F4: Very High / Peak. The active preset appears in the window title. Presets reset to Normal on restart.
- Close the window normally to exit. Edits are also saved after each change when a user data directory is available.

These changes have not yet been play-tested on Windows.

## Early mode controls

- Survival and Hardcore: mine logs and blocks to collect them. Slot counts are displayed on the hotbar. Leaves give berries; press **F** to eat one when hungry.
- Press **C** to turn one collected log into four planks (slot 6). This is a temporary shortcut, not a crafting menu.
- Red squares show health; yellow squares show hunger. In water, hold Space to swim up and Shift to descend. Running out of air, falling too far, or starving can cause damage.
- Creative: double-tap Space to fly; blocks are unlimited. Flying is disabled in the other modes.
- Hardcore worlds require local saving. On death, the world is retained but cannot be reopened for play. It is not a spectator mode.
