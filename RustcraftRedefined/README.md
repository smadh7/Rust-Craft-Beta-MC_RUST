# Rustcraft Redefined

An unofficial voxel sandbox in Rust and C++. The goal is to match the behaviour of
Minecraft: Java Edition 26.2 ("Chaos Cubed") as closely as a clean-room project can.
It is **not** there yet: this document says exactly what works today and what does not.

Not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft. No game code, textures, sounds or text from
Minecraft are used; every texture is generated by code in `src/art/`.

## Running it

| Platform | How | Tested |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Browser (WebGL 2) | Open `web/index.html`, or serve the `web/` folder | Yes. Headless Chromium runs drive the menus, survival play, mobs, saving and reloading |
| Windows 10/11 x64 | Run `RustcraftRedefined.exe` from the repository root | **No.** It is cross-compiled and links cleanly, but has never been run on Windows |
| Linux, macOS | Use the browser build | Native window not implemented |

See [BUILD.md](BUILD.md) to build from source and [CONTROLS.md](CONTROLS.md) for keys and commands.

## Architecture

```
cpp/            freestanding C++17 core behind a C ABI (no libc, no allocation)
  vc_terrain    Perlin/fBm noise, multi-noise terrain density, caves
  vc_mesh       section mesher: data-driven block shapes, smooth light, ambient occlusion, fluids
src/            the game, in Rust, with no third-party crates
  world/        block registry and states, chunks (-64..320), biomes, generation, lighting,
                placement rules, and the block simulation (fluids, growth, decay)
  item/         items, tools, food, fuel, block drops, crafting and smelting recipes
  game/         20 Hz simulation, player physics, inventory, mobs, explosions, geysers,
                menus and container screens, HUD, commands, world saving
  gfx/          OpenGL 3.3 core / WebGL 2 abstraction, chunk renderer, sky, GUI batch
  art/          procedural textures for blocks, items and mobs, bitmap font, GUI atlas
  audio.rs      sound effects synthesised from noise and sine waves
  platform/     browser host bindings, Win32 + WGL window, storage
web/            page and JavaScript host for the WebAssembly build
tools/          build scripts and the headless test and screenshot harness
```

Rust owns the game state. The C++ core does the heavy numeric work (noise, terrain, meshing)
and is compiled by `build.rs` for whichever target is being built, including `wasm32`.

## What works

- **World**: height -64 to 320, 16x16x16 sections, streaming around the player, 40 biomes chosen
  from five climate noises (oceans, rivers, beaches, plains, forests, taiga, jungle, desert,
  badlands, swamps, mountains and peaks, mushroom fields, ...), 3D caves, deepslate, bedrock,
  ores by depth, eight tree shapes plus giant mushrooms, flowers and plants, snow and ice in cold biomes.
- **Cave biomes**: lush caves, dripstone caves, deep dark, and the 26.2 **sulfur caves** with
  sulfur and cinnabar, sulfur spikes and sulfur springs.
- **Blocks**: 488 blocks with 7,254 block states: cubes, pillars, slabs, stairs with corner
  shapes, fences, walls, panes, doors, trapdoors, ladders, torches, snow layers, crops, fluids.
  This includes the full 26.2 sulfur and cinnabar sets.
- **Lighting**: sky and block light with face-aware propagation, incremental updates, smooth
  lighting and ambient occlusion.
- **Rendering**: texture array with mipmaps, cut-out and translucent passes, animated water and
  lava, biome-blended colours, fog, day/night sky with sun, moon and stars, block-breaking
  cracks, particles, the held item in first person.
- **Player**: movement uses the vanilla constants and is tested against them (walking 4.317 m/s,
  sprinting 5.612 m/s, jump height 1.249 blocks), plus sneaking with edge protection, swimming,
  ladders, step-up, creative flight and spectator mode.
- **Survival**: timed mining with tool tiers and speeds, tool wear, block drops, dropped items
  that can be picked up, health, hunger and saturation, eating, drowning, fall, lava, cactus,
  magma and void damage, death and respawn.
- **Items and crafting**: about 600 items (every block plus 117 tools, materials and foods),
  over 400 crafting recipes in the 2x2 and 3x3 grids, furnaces with fuel and smelting, chests,
  buckets, hoes, shovels (paths), axes (log stripping), shears, bone meal, flint and steel.
- **Inventory screens**: survival inventory, crafting table, furnace, chest, and a creative item
  picker with category tabs and search; shift-click, right-click splitting, number-key swaps.
- **Simulation**: water and lava flow, fall, recede and (for water) form new sources; lava and
  water make obsidian, cobblestone and stone; grass spreads and dies; crops grow on hydrated
  farmland; saplings grow into trees; leaves decay; sugar cane and cactus grow; sand and gravel
  fall; TNT explodes with blast resistance and chain reactions.
- **Mobs**: pig, cow, sheep, chicken, zombie, spider, creeper and the Sulfur Cube, with
  box models, wandering, fleeing, food luring, hostile pursuit, melee combat, creeper
  explosions, zombies burning in daylight, natural spawning and despawning, loot, sheep
  shearing and milking. A peaceful difficulty is available in the options.
- **26.2 features**: see [RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md). The Sulfur Cube absorbs a full block and
  turns into a ball with one of twelve physics archetypes; it can be sheared, bucketed, fed
  slime balls, splits in two when killed, and carries TNT that can be lit. Potent Sulfur over
  magma or lava under water forms a geyser.
- **Sound**: effects synthesised in code (no audio files) for digging, placing, footsteps,
  item pickup, hits, explosions, lava meeting water, eating, the Sulfur Cube, geysers and menu
  clicks, with a volume slider. The browser plays them through Web Audio. On Windows they go
  through `PlaySound`, which plays one sound at a time and ignores pitch and volume; like the
  rest of the Windows build this has not been run.
- **Menus**: title screen, world list, world creation with seed and game mode, pause menu,
  options (FOV, render distance, sensitivity, brightness, GUI scale, view bobbing, difficulty, sound),
  death screen, chat with command history.
- **Saving**: worlds are saved to the game data folder (Windows) or IndexedDB (browser) when
  quitting to the title, every five minutes, and when the tab is hidden or the window closed.

## What does not work yet

Measured against the stated goal, large parts of the game are still missing:

- Music, ambient sound and mob voices: only short synthesised effects exist
- The Nether and the End, and all structures (villages, mineshafts, strongholds, ...)
- Redstone, pistons, fire, rails and minecarts, boats, beds and sleeping, weather
- Armour, experience, enchanting, potions and status effects, bows and other ranged weapons
- Most mobs: only eight exist. No villagers or trading, no skeletons, endermen, slimes, bosses,
  no breeding or baby animals
- Multiplayer, the 26.2 Friends List, the Vulkan renderer option, advancements, data packs,
  resource packs, most commands
- A few hundred blocks and items of the full game

Known inaccuracies in what does exist:

- Worlds do **not** match Minecraft seeds: the terrain generator is an original design.
- Saves use this project's own format; Minecraft cannot read them and vice versa.
- Mob pathfinding is a straight line with jumping; mobs do not plan routes around obstacles.
- Leaves placed by hand decay like natural ones, because leaf blocks have no `persistent` state.
- Stalactites that lose their support drop as items instead of falling and dealing damage.
- Sulfur Cube: the archetype numbers are estimates from the published descriptions; minecart
  riding and dispenser interaction are missing. Geysers have no nausea gas.
- Dying drops the inventory, but there is no experience to lose and no bed to respawn at.

## Tests

`cargo test` runs 76 unit tests: noise, registry, generation, lighting, physics, block rules,
items and recipes, inventory logic, furnaces, saving, fluid flow, growth and decay, mob
behaviour, Sulfur Cube ball physics and sound synthesis.
`tools/shot.mjs` drives the browser build headlessly: it can click through the menus, type,
hold keys and mouse buttons, query game state and capture screenshots.
